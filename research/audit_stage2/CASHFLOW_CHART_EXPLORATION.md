# Cash-flow and stock/bond chart exploration

This chart pack adds five research views, generated by the Linux BOT from the SHA-pinned QQQ/SPY source CSV:

1. **Relative-strength "mutual chase":** monthly QQQ/SPY rotation. Each month's trailing 12-month price return, known at the previous month close, selects the next month's holding. It is not a stock/bond rotation.
2. **Flexible withdrawal calendar:** the existing illustrative schedule, with no events during the first 12 months, later step-up/down withdrawals and a later deposit.
3. **Stock-to-bond capital-flow view:** the initial USD 100,000 split into an illustrative zero-coupon Treasury/STRIPS purchase cost and the maturity face payment plus remaining growth sleeve. The cost is derived from a 10-year par-yield reference, not an executable quote.
4. **Stock-to-bond glidepath:** compares eight ten-year target conversions of the initial equity sleeve: 30%, 40%, 50%, 60%, 70%, 80%, 90% and 100%. Each target is reached via an even annual transfer over ten years. The chart shows equity/bond weights, not investment returns.
5. **Cash-flow outcome ranges:** terminal-balance ranges with no cashflows and with the illustrative deferred/step-up schedule over the same 211 overlapping ten-year windows. Median withdrawals and 95th-percentile shortfall are shown too.

## Output

The workflow uploads an HTML report (index.html), independent SVG charts, methodology/result JSON and cashflow_outcome_ranges.csv. SVGs are dependency-free vector graphics.

## Data and interpretation

- Input: artifacts/pulse_v2/data_market_data.csv; the calculator checks its SHA-256.
- Window count: 211 overlapping ten-year price windows from 1999-03 to 2026-09.
- Equity fields are price-only; no dividend reinvestment, taxes, trading friction, options, leverage or live orders.
- Treasury floor cost is not a historical STRIPS price or executable quote; bond market value, duration, reinvestment and early-sale liquidity are not simulated.
- The 30%-100% glidepath chart shows target weights only. For example, a 60% target ends at 40% stocks / 60% bonds in year 10. It must not be read as a backtested stock/bond allocation strategy; bond price/total-return history is required to compare performance.
- The withdrawal amounts are test values, not spending defaults or recommendations.

## Expanded offline interactive glidepath dashboard

The separate `interactive_dashboard.html` is a single-file, offline research UI built from the 768 scenario summaries and 8,448 year-specific summaries. It now offers thirteen coordinated views:

1. Four selected-year comparison charts: median NAV by target, drawdown-to-date by target, cumulative time-weighted CAGR by target, and withdrawal-shortfall windows by target. P05 NAV is displayed in the metric cards, fan chart and cohort tail summaries.
2. A return-versus-drawdown scatter for the currently selected year and cash-flow mode; choosing a point updates the selected strategy.
3. The ten-year glidepath with six distinct timing curves.
4. A historical NAV distribution fan (P05/P50/P95 plus the worst window) for the selected strategy.
5. An underwater-style path for median and worst cumulative maximum drawdown.
6. An 8-target by 6-timing heatmap. The user can switch the heatmap metric among median NAV, P05 NAV, cumulative CAGR, drawdown-to-date and shortfall-window count. Clicking a cell updates the other views.
7. A six-rule timing comparison table and same-strategy cash-flow comparison.
8. Local CSV export for the selected 48 target/timing combinations or the complete 8,448 annual-summary rows.
9. A tenth-year outcome histogram for the selected strategy, switchable among terminal NAV, ten-year time-weighted CAGR, daily maximum drawdown and cumulative withdrawal shortfall. It is built from all 157 window rows in the replay CSV, then stored compactly as `window_distribution_summary.json` rather than embedding the entire per-window ledger.
10. A historical start-year sensitivity chart. It groups the real rolling windows by the calendar year of the window start and displays median/P05 terminal NAV, median/P05 CAGR, median/worst drawdown and shortfall-window counts. Clicking a cohort bar shows the group’s window count, observed date span and selected tail metrics. The companion `window_start_cohort_summary.json` checks that the year groups conserve all 157 windows for every strategy.

The fan is a descriptive percentile range across overlapping historical windows, **not** a forecast confidence interval. The histogram shows the distribution shape of the actual window-level observations; every bin count must reconcile to the valid row count for that strategy/metric. Start-year groups are also overlapping windows; their purpose is to expose start-period dependence, not to claim that the number of poor windows is the probability of a future loss. Heatmap colors rank only the selected metric within the currently filtered 48-cell set; a high value is not universally desirable (for example, fewer shortfall windows is better). The return/drawdown scatter is filtered to the selected year and flow, so it intentionally shows at most 48 points.

The interaction pattern takes inspiration from linked brushing and cross-filtering rather than placing unrelated charts on one page. Reference techniques:
- Observable, [linked brushing across views](https://observablehq.com/blog/linked-brushing): selecting one subset and reflecting it in linked charts.
- D3, [brush interactions](https://d3js.org/d3-brush): point/region selection and cross-filtering interaction primitives.
- Marketcalls, [OpenStatz quant tearsheets](https://github.com/marketcalls/openstatz): an offline single-file portfolio report with distinct performance, risk, drawdown and return-distribution sections.

These references provide design patterns, not validation of the glidepath strategy. The research UI remains offline, and all displayed numbers come from the pinned replay output; it must not silently interpolate missing annual data or treat historical sample frequencies as future probabilities.
