### 1.4 Nominal Break-Even vs. Real Purchasing-Power Break-Even

The prompt explicitly mandates: *"Secondary objective: report terminal inflation-adjusted purchasing power (`Real_NAV_10`) separately. Do not conflate nominal break-even with real break-even."*

Let $B_0 = \$100,000$ denote initial capital and $\pi_{10}$ denote the annualized compounded U.S. Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation rate over the 10-year horizon $[t_0, t_{10}]$. Grounded in the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis FRED series `T10YIE` as of October 16, 2026 ([FRED 10-Year Breakeven Inflation Rate](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Ffred.stlouisfed.org%2Fseries%2Ft10yie)), market-implied 10-year breakeven inflation is $\pi_{\text{be}} = 2.35\%$ per annum, while the 10-year nominal constant-maturity Treasury yield is $y_{\text{nom}} = 5.21\%$ ([Convex FRED DGS10](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fconvextrade.com%2Fmetrics%2Fdgs10)), implying an ex-ante 10-year TIPS real yield of $y_{\text{real}} = y_{\text{nom}} - \pi_{\text{be}} = 2.86\%$ per annum.

The relationship between terminal nominal wealth $\text{NAV}_{10}$ and terminal inflation-adjusted purchasing power $\text{Real\_NAV}_{10}$ (expressed in constant Year-0 USD) is:

$$\text{Real\_NAV}_{10} = \frac{\text{NAV}_{10}}{(1 + \pi_{10})^{10}}$$

Consequently, achieving **nominal break-even** ($\text{NAV}_{10} = \$100,000$) results in a **real purchasing-power loss** whenever $\pi_{10} > 0$:
- Under baseline market-implied inflation ($\pi_{10} = 2.35\%$ p.a., cumulative price-level factor $(1.0235)^{10} = 1.26146$), a nominal terminal value of $\text{NAV}_{10} = \$100,000$ has a real purchasing power of only **$\text{Real\_NAV}_{10} = \$79,273$** (`-20.73%` real drawdown). To achieve **real break-even** ($\text{Real\_NAV}_{10} \ge \$100,000$), terminal nominal wealth must reach at least **$\text{NAV}_{10}^{\text{real-be}}(\text{2.35\%}) = \$126,146$**.
- Under a moderate inflation regime ($\pi_{10} = 3.50\%$ p.a., factor $(1.035)^{10} = 1.41060$), nominal break-even (`$100,000`) equals **$\text{Real\_NAV}_{10} = \$70,892$** (`-29.11%` real loss), and real break-even requires **$\text{NAV}_{10}^{\text{real-be}}(\text{3.50\%}) = \$141,060$**.
- Under a stagflationary shock regime ($\pi_{10} = 5.00\%$ p.a., factor $(1.05)^{10} = 1.62889$), nominal break-even (`$100,000`) equals **$\text{Real\_NAV}_{10} = \$61,391$** (`-38.61%` real loss), and real break-even requires **$\text{NAV}_{10}^{\text{real-be}}(\text{5.00\%}) = \$162,889$**.

By combining `$60,500` in 10-year Treasury STRIPS (which locks in `$100,000` nominal par at Year 10) with `$14,500` in 10-year TIPS (whose principal scales directly with $(1 + \pi_{10})^{10}$ plus a `2.86%` real coupon) and `$25,000` in unleveraged `SPY`/`QQQ` equities, the recommended architecture guarantees the `$100,000` nominal floor unconditionally at maturity while reaching real break-even ($\text{Real\_NAV}_{10} \ge \$100,000$) even if the equity sleeve experiences a `-80%` terminal crash (`$100,590` from STRIPS/cash + `$24,230` from TIPS + `$5,000` from residual equities = `$129,820` nominal = **`$102,912` real** at `2.35%` CPI).

## 4. Dynamic Sizing & Reallocation Formulas

To satisfy the primary objective ($\max \Pr(\text{NAV}_{10} \ge B_0)$ with $B_0 = \$100,000$ at $T = 10$ years) while preserving upside participation and real purchasing power, all sizing and reallocation rules are derived from first-principles **Zero-Coupon Floor Immunized Risk Budgeting (Ratchet Constant-Proportion Cushion Architecture)**. No short-option, VXX-short, or Black-Scholes synthetic overlay is permitted to encumber the principal floor.

### 4.1 Mathematical Variable Definitions

Let $t \in [0, T]$ denote elapsed time in years ($T = 10.0$, or monthly decision index $m \in \{0, 1, \dots, 120\}$ with $\Delta t = 1/12$):
- $B_0 = \$100,000$: Initial nominal capital and target Year-10 nominal floor.
- $\pi(t) = \text{CPI}_t / \text{CPI}_0$: Cumulative realized Consumer Price Index ratio from $t=0$ to $t$, defining the real break-even floor $B_{\text{real}}(t) = B_0 \cdot \pi(t)$.
- $y_{\text{strip}}(t)$: Prevailing semi-annually compounded yield-to-maturity on the U.S. Treasury Principal STRIPS maturing at $T = 10$ (`Oct 2036`), with remaining maturity $\tau(t) = T - t$.
- $P_{\text{strip}}(t) = \left(1 + \frac{y_{\text{strip}}(t)}{2}\right)^{-2\tau(t)}$: Market discount factor per `$1.00` of Year-10 par value for the maturing Treasury Principal STRIPS.
- $N_{\text{strip}}(t)$: Par face value (in USD at maturity $T$) of 10-year U.S. Treasury Principal STRIPS held in Sleeve A1. At $t = 0$, $N_{\text{strip}}(0) = \$100,000$, purchased at ask cost $V_{A1}(0) = N_{\text{strip}}(0) \cdot P_{\text{strip}}^{\text{ask}}(0) \approx \$60,145$ (plus `$355` fee/cash buffer = `$60,500`).
- $V_{A1}(t) = N_{\text{strip}}(t) \cdot P_{\text{strip}}^{\text{bid}}(t)$: Current liquidation mark-to-market value of Sleeve A1 (STRIPS), while its **hold-to-maturity terminal value** is deterministically $F_{A1}(T) = N_{\text{strip}}(t) \ge \$100,000$.
- $V_{A2}(t)$: Market value of Sleeve A2 (10-year TIPS + short-term T-Bill tax/liquidity reserve; initial $V_{A2}(0) = \$14,500$).
- $V_B(t) = q_{\text{SPY}}(t) S_{\text{SPY}}(t) + q_{\text{QQQ}}(t) S_{\text{QQQ}}(t)$: Market value of Sleeve B (unleveraged equity growth core; initial $V_B(0) = \$25,000$, split `60%` `SPY`/`VOO` = `$15,000` and `40%` `QQQ`/`QQQM` = `$10,000`).
- $\text{NAV}(t) = V_{A1}(t) + V_{A2}(t) + V_B(t) + \text{Cash}(t)$: Total portfolio net liquidation value at time $t$.
- $\tau_{\text{oid}}(t)$: Annual accrued federal tax liability on STRIPS/TIPS Original Issue Discount (OID) in a taxable account ($\tau_{\text{oid}}(t) = 0$ in a tax-advantaged/exempt account).

### 4.2 Exact Dynamic Sizing, Cushion, and Ratchet Reallocation Formulas

1. **Hard Terminal Floor Invariant (Hold-to-Maturity Lock)**:
   At all times $t \in [0, T]$, the par face value of Treasury Principal STRIPS in Sleeve A1 must satisfy:

   $$N_{\text{strip}}(t) \ge B_0 + \sum_{k=\lceil t \rceil}^{10} \widehat{\tau}_{\text{oid}}(k)$$

   where $\widehat{\tau}_{\text{oid}}(k)$ is the remaining scheduled OID tax reserve (`$0` in tax-exempt accounts; funded via Sleeve A2 T-Bills in taxable accounts). **Sleeve A1 STRIPS are never sold to fund equity purchases**, insulating the `$100,000` Year-10 nominal payout from interim interest-rate mark-to-market fluctuations.

2. **Economic Surplus Cushion $C(t)$ and Dynamic Equity Target $E^*(t)$**:
   Define the discounted present value of the required floor $F_{\text{req}}(t) = B_0 \cdot P_{\text{strip}}^{\text{ask}}(t) + R_{\text{tax}}(t)$, where $R_{\text{tax}}(t)$ is the present value of remaining OID tax reserves. The portfolio's **Investable Surplus Cushion** above the hard floor is:

   $$C(t) = \max\!\big(0,\; \text{NAV}(t) - F_{\text{req}}(t)\big) = \max\!\big(0,\; V_{A2}^{\text{free}}(t) + V_B(t) + \text{Cash}(t)\big)$$

   Using a conservative risk-budget multiplier $m(\mathcal{R}_t) \in [0.50, 1.00]$ applied solely to the non-STRIPS surplus capital $S(t) = V_{A2}^{\text{free}}(t) + V_B(t) + \text{Cash}(t)$ (where $m(\mathcal{R}_t)$ never exceeds `1.00`, guaranteeing **zero leverage** and **zero sale of Sleeve A1 STRIPS**), the target equity allocation $E^*(t)$ is:

   $$E^*(t) = \min\!\Big(S(t),\; m(\mathcal{R}_t) \cdot S(t)\Big), \quad \text{with } m(\mathcal{R}_t) = \begin{cases} 0.633 & \text{if } \mathcal{R}_t = \text{CALM} \quad (\text{e.g., } \$25,000 / \$39,500 = 63.3\% \text{ of surplus}) \\ 0.750 & \text{if } \mathcal{R}_t = \text{DEEP\_DRAWDOWN\_VALUATION} \quad (\text{DD}_{\text{SPY}, 252} \le -0.25 \text{ and } t \le 7.0) \\ 0.500 & \text{if } \mathcal{R}_t = \text{LATE\_HORIZON\_LOCK} \quad (t > 8.0 \text{ and } \text{NAV}(t) \ge 1.50 B_0) \end{cases}$$

3. **High-Water-Mark Ratchet Floor Formula (Locking Real Purchasing Power & Equity Gains)**:
   Whenever total portfolio value reaches a new 20%-step milestone above the inflation-adjusted floor—specifically, whenever equity sleeve gains allow locking a higher permanent Year-10 floor without reducing equity weight below `15%` of NAV—the target Year-10 floor $B_{\text{ratchet}}(t)$ ratchets upward:

   $$B_{\text{ratchet}}(t) = \max\!\left(B_{\text{ratchet}}(t^-),\; B_0 \cdot \pi(t) \cdot \mathbb{I}\big(V_B(t) - \Delta V_{A1} \ge 0.20 \cdot \text{NAV}(t)\big),\; B_0 + 0.50 \max\big(0, V_B(t) - 1.60 V_B(0)\big)\right)$$

   When $B_{\text{ratchet}}(t) > N_{\text{strip}}(t^-)$, the portfolio harvests $\Delta V_B = \big(B_{\text{ratchet}}(t) - N_{\text{strip}}(t^-)\big) \cdot P_{\text{strip}}^{\text{ask}}(t)$ from Sleeve B (`SPY`/`QQQ`) and purchases additional 10-year (matching Oct 2036 maturity) Treasury STRIPS or TIPS, permanently raising the guaranteed Year-10 payout from `$100,000` toward the real break-even floor ($\$126,146+$).

4. **Internal Equity Sleeve Split (Mitigating QQQ Concentration Risk)**:
   Within Sleeve B ($V_B(t)$), target weights are capped at $w_{\text{SPY}} = 0.60$ (`60%` broad S&P 500) and $w_{\text{QQQ}} = 0.40$ (`40%` Nasdaq-100), so single-index tech concentration never exceeds `10.0%–12.0%` of total portfolio NAV at initiation.

### 4.3 Explicit No-Trade Bands & Hard Circuit-Breaker Conditions

To prevent turnover drag, bid-ask slippage, and tax realization (the exact flaws that destroyed `protected.ts` and `backtest.ts`), the architecture enforces six explicit **No-Trade / Circuit-Breaker Conditions**:

1. **No-Trade Rebalancing Deadband (`|w_B(t) - w_B^*(t)| <= 5.0%`)**:
   No rebalancing trade between Sleeve A2 (TIPS/T-Bills) and Sleeve B (`SPY`/`QQQ`) is executed unless the absolute drift of equity share of surplus $|V_B(t)/S(t) - m(\mathcal{R}_t)|$ exceeds **`5.0` percentage points** (`0.05`) AND at least **`90` calendar days** have elapsed since the last rebalance (maximum `4` checks per year).
2. **Hard Prohibition on Selling Sleeve A1 STRIPS (`CB-FLOOR-IMMUNITY`)**:
   If $\text{NAV}(t)$ declines due to a simultaneous rise in interest rates and fall in equities (as in 2022), **zero units of Sleeve A1 STRIPS may be sold**. Because Sleeve A1 is held to exact Year-10 maturity, interim duration mark-to-market drawdowns reverse automatically to `$100,000` par at $T = 10$.
3. **Hard Prohibition on Unverified / Negative-EV Derivatives (`CB-NO-SYNTHETIC-OPTIONS`)**:
   Consistent with `SharedContractEngine v4.4` gates `F2`, `F4`, `F6`, and `F8`:
   - **No QQQ Put Ratio (`1L:NS` or `NL:1S`)**: Blocked unconditionally whenever `VerifiedSourceStore.verifiedExitEpisodeCount < 100` (`BLOCKED_NO_VERIFIED_EPISODES`), whenever `maxTailLossAtZero > 0.01 * NAV` (`BLOCKED_ZERO_WHOLE_UNITS`), or whenever intraday broker margin rules are unverified (`F6 MARGIN_UNVERIFIED`).
   - **No Naked Short QQQ Calls (`V22` `N_SHORT = 10`)**: Blocked unconditionally for a `$100,000` capital-preservation account because 10 short QQQ calls represent `1,000` shares $\approx \$718,960$ notional (`7.19x` portfolio leverage), creating uncapped right-tail loss and early-assignment dividend risk.
   - **No Short VXX Stock or VXX Options**: Blocked whenever borrow cost $> 3.0\%$ p.a. (`currently 12.50%`), recall status $\ne \text{NONE}$, or VX term slope $S \le 5.0\%$.
4. **Data Freshness & Reconciliation Circuit Breaker (`CB-F1-F7-GATE`)**:
   Adopted directly from `SharedContractEngine v4.4` (`evaluateDecision`, `lines 2923-2956`): if any price or yield timestamp has `age < 0` (future timestamp), `age > 4` calendar days (`DATA STALE`), cross-asset timestamp gap `|spotTermGap| > 2` days (`AS-OF MIXED`), any required ledger field is `EMPTY` (`NEEDS_LEDGER_INPUT`), or external broker statement net liquidation value diverges from ledger-derived NAV by `$> $0.01` (`BLOCKED_STATEMENT_RECONCILIATION_MISMATCH`), the system immediately locks in `PAUSE / HOLD` (`0` orders permitted).
5. **Late-Horizon Equity Add Lockout (`CB-YEAR-8-10-LOCKOUT`)**:
   During the final 24 months ($t \in [8.0, 10.0]$, remaining horizon $\tau < 2.0$ years), **no net transfers from Sleeve A2 (TIPS/T-Bills) into Sleeve B (`SPY`/`QQQ`) are permitted** (`maxBuyEquity = 0`), preventing late-cycle equity top-buying right before the Year-10 evaluation date.
6. **Minimum Trade Notional Filter (`CB-MIN-TICKET`)**:
   Any calculated rebalance order with gross notional $< \$1,500$ (`1.5%` of initial NAV) or bid-ask spread $> 15$ bps is suppressed (`NO_TRADE`).

In that case, Sleeve A1 must be replaced by tax-exempt AAA pre-refunded/escrowed-to-maturity 10-year Municipal Zero-Coupon Bonds or BOXX/Treasury total-return structures coupled with direct TIPS in the tax-sheltered space.
